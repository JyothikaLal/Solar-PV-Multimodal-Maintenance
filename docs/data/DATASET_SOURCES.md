## Dataset Relationship

TECNALIA and RaptorMaps are independent datasets.

They will not be paired by:
- asset
- module
- timestamp
- physical equipment
- image
- telemetry record

They will be trained and evaluated independently and combined only at the
decision-level fusion stage.
